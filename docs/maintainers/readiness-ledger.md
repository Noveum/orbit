## Accountability and status rules

Every owner role maps to a named person in an access-controlled release record. Public `principal:` and `record:` values are stable references to those access-controlled records, not invented names. The structured fields use `implementation:record:<key>`, `test:record:<key>`, `gate:record:<key>`, `docs:record:<key>`, `risk:record:<key>`, `approver:principal:<key>`, and `authority:principal:<key>`. A demonstrably non-behavioral change may use `test-na:record:<key>;justification=record:<key>;approver=principal:<key>` instead of a failing test.

`Open` rows use the explicit placeholders `pending:open` and, only when security authority is not required, `not-required`. No other placeholder is valid. `Ready for closure`, `Closed`, and `Accepted P1 exception` require implementation, change, release-gate, documentation, residual-risk, decision, and independent-approver records. A decision is `decision:record:<key>;implementation=principal:<key>;finding=principal:<key>;approver=principal:<key>`. Its three principals must differ, the finding principal must equal the accountable owner reference, and the approver must equal the independent approver reference.

Every P0 is `Open` and cannot use `Accepted P1 exception`. `Security authority required` is reviewed explicitly for every finding. A security-required transition needs an authority reference; a non-security transition must use `not-required`. An accepted P1 exception needs an expiry after the checker verification date and must match its finding's owner, residual-risk, decision, approver, and authority records.
